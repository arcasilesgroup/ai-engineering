Verdict

reading, not a verdict

What follows is a reading; nothing here approves or refuses the decision.

The decision is sound in shape: a record that grants no authority, a refusal that changes a measured count, and a blast radius closed by its own words. The authority of the model does not come from the record, and the record does not pretend it does. What the reading finds is that on this tree the decision delivers the refusal and the register stance today, and the ADR only after a repair no element of this decision is allowed to make — so the person who runs this goal receives the smallest half now and is told the other half is another spec's work. That is honest, and it is also the whole story that matters about this choice.

Recommendation

  1. Land the reachable half now, in one commit: the labelled refusal in the ai-goal corpus for the no-route recording case, the skill_eval register entry argued to the 350 baseline, and the receipt that the run prints 0.
  2. Record the proposed ADR as the second step, gated on the approved repair of the inherited record — and say so in the goal's own page so the blocked step names its unblocker, as the spec already does.
  3. Leave the inherited history standing; rewriting it is not this decision's to make, and the spec's refusal to do so is the correct boundary.

One first step: add the labelled refusal for the no-route recording case to the ai-goal corpus and move the skill_eval baseline to 350 in policy/pilot-register.toml in the same commit, with the reason given — the only part of this decision that can be checked today.
