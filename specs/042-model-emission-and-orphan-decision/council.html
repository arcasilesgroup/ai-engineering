Round three — the chairman

The lenses agree on five things. First: every round-one gap is closed or honestly narrowed — the volume sentence is exact, the four model states are named and separately counted, the rule-12 relabel is the right direction, the consumer definition is mechanical, and tier_model is a model string. Second: the tree facts re-derive byte-for-byte. Third: the fallback sentence in B-042-1 is now exactly what the router does (medium when configured, else default_tier, else the empty string). Fourth: the register follows the skill-sequence.toml pattern and its reason citations check out (031/B-031-2, 041, 037 rows 7 and 14). Fifth: the fail-closed commitment is kept everywhere.

Where they clash: whether the model distribution is a deliverable or a placeholder at ship. One reading: the spec says honestly blank, so a column of missing/ undetermined is correct first output. The other: the marquee claim is repeated without the blankness in the same breath, and the enabling adapter change is owned by nobody in the increment. They also split on the escalation's reach: the person channel is named and real, but "hands the decision to a person" is still a message to the machine.

The blind spots the cross-read caught: the chain-hook setdefault line raises TypeError on the only payload that exists today (G-R1); the digest's relabel depends on an escalated=True field no behaviour creates while retiring the only forcing signal rule 12 has (G-R2); command events' model is undetermined-by- construction and the example event dresses it as populated (G-R3); every arithmetic claim in B-042-4 is off the guard's real counting (G-R4); and "no latency field exists" is contradicted by the 8,010 events that carry data.ms (G-R5).

Verdict: the revision closes the five round-one gaps and its honesty fixes are real — but the new material introduces five defects of the same family: the spec's own examples and arithmetic contradict the mechanisms they illustrate. A reader copying the examples into tests or implementations carries the contradictions in.

Recommendation: keep the four behaviours as shaped; fix the new material. In B-042-2 replace the quoted line with the guarded form and say explicitly that the reported-actual state on command events reads undetermined on the wired surface until an adapter change this spec names but does not make. In B-042-4 add one sentence declaring the blocked event's data carries escalated=True, and re-derive the digest claim from the guard's denial index (three rows, not two). In the examples, make the escalation's count window-bounded and its signature tool-prefixed, and show one post-change digest line. Leave the rule-12 flag in place until a person-action exists to replace it.

First step: two lines — the guarded model pass-through in B-042-2, and the escalated=True field on the denied event in B-042-4 — because until those two lines exist, the chain half of the distribution cannot run and the digest's relabel reads a field no event carries.
