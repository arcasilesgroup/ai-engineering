03Checker false positives

208 glyph-collision reports · not painted collisions

The collector’s shown helper checks computed display, visibility and opacity, then a bounding box larger than 1px; it does not check whether a parent native details is closed. The live diagnostics markup is a closed <details class="workspace-diagnostics"> containing a summary and list. Its list rows are not painted until the user expands it. The 208 reports are therefore collector artefacts; this report does not claim actual text collisions.

Reported warningEvidence and disposition
Empty heading(s)False positive. The accessible-name helper checks ARIA name attributes, title, images, SVG titles and value, but not the heading’s own text. The rendered headings contain text.
Content outside landmarks: <div>False positive. This is the non-text .app-shell grid wrapper around semantic <aside> and <main> landmarks.

04Interior warning: instrument, not card drift

The sole interior finding compares .summary-item.attention with .summary-item and infers a 22px lead for one and 12px for the other. At 375px all four rendered summary cards share 16px padding, 22px values and 12px labels.

False positive · text-role extraction mismatch

Source inspection confirms the collector searches child elements for direct text nodes longer than one character, then uses the largest font size it finds. It does not identify the semantic .summary-value role; a short numeric text node can be skipped, leaving the 12px label as the inferred lead. The warning’s inferred 12px base therefore does not reflect a difference in the rendered cards. No interior defect is claimed.

Retained note-level observations

The final output also contains nine single-sided stroke inventory entries. They are note-severity observations, not proximity-ratio findings. The checker cannot determine from a stroke alone whether it is a separator or a structural edge, so their design intent remains unadjudicated; they are retained as review notes, not passed findings.
