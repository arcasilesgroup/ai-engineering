03Kept on purpose

The journey dots encode step size. The diameters are 27, 32, 37, 42 and 47. The three small rungs miss a 44px thumb target. Growing them would flatten the ladder. The reason sits next to the formula.

04Fixed

The page no longer scrolls sideways

Seven dots need 264px. The lines between them were not allowed under 8px, so the row ran to 312 and the document scrolled 11px at 320. The floor is now 4px. At 320 the row is 288 of 288.

The disclosure was 19.4px tall. It now has 13px of padding on each side, so the hit box clears 44px. That padding is inside a closed card, so the collapsed page does not move.
